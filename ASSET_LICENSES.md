# Asset sources and repository rules

The game uses third-party art packs under their authors' terms. Raw pack files are excluded from this Git repository by `.gitignore`. Do not remove those exclusions or commit the contents of the asset folders.

To run the complete game from a fresh clone, obtain the packs from their creators and restore the matching files locally in the paths below. The game source expects those paths and filenames. The asset packs may be used inside a game, but their raw files must not be re-uploaded or redistributed as a separate asset collection.

- **SHADOW Series — The Free Assets**, Escape Pixell: https://escape-pixel.itch.io/shadow-series-full-bundle
  - Restore the free Hallokin and Blind Huntress sprite sheets under `assets/heroes/shadow/`.
  - Attribution is appreciated; the creator suggests the credit name “Escape Pixell”.
- **Tiny RPG Character Asset Pack v1.03 — Free Soldier & Orc**, Zerie: https://zerie.itch.io/tiny-rpg-character-asset-pack
  - Restore Soldier, Orc, and projectile art under `assets/enemies/`.
  - Commercial game use and modifications are allowed; re-uploading the raw assets is not. Credit is appreciated but not required.
- **Gabo's Adventurers Pack I — Hobbit**, oisougabo: https://oisougabo.itch.io/gap-i
  - Restore Hobbit animation frames under `assets/enemies/Hobbit/`.
  - Commercial game use and modifications are allowed; redistribution of the pack, whole or partial, is not. The creator asks for credit.
- **Pixel Fire Asset Pack v1.2**, Devkidd: https://devkidd.itch.io/pixel-fire-asset-pack
  - Restore torch animation frames under `assets/effects/fire/`.
  - Personal and commercial project use is allowed; standalone redistribution is not. Attribution is appreciated.
- **Blood FX — BloodFX Batch 1**, jasontomlee: https://jasontomlee.itch.io/blood-fx
  - Restore the used effect frames under `assets/effects/blood/`.
  - The asset page identifies CC BY 4.0, requires attribution, and states that the asset files may not be redistributed: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The castle backgrounds and blended game logo in `assets/` are project artwork. The procedural sound effects and ambient loop are generated by `main.gd` and use no external sound pack.

No open-source license has been selected for the game's own source code. Until the author adds one, do not assume permission to reuse it.
